Tahoe Paws & Shores 🐾

Find exactly where your pup can splash, dig, and swim legally around Lake Tahoe.

Know Before You Go

Tahoe fines for off-leash dogs on standard public beaches can be steep. Always keep your dog on a 6-foot leash unless you are explicitly inside a designated off-leash zone, and pack out all waste!

Around the lake

Dog-friendly beaches

South ShoreLeash Required

Kiva Beach

Terrain: Sandy & Pebbles

One of the most famous dog spots. Located near the Tallac Historic Site. Dogs are strictly allowed east of Tallac Point only (don't wander into the protected wetlands west of it!).

Local Tip: Parking is free but fills up incredibly fast on hot summer weekends.

South ShoreOff-Leash Allowed

Regan Beach (Dog Area)

Terrain: Grassy & Shallow Entry

South Tahoe's oldest beach has an enclosed, dedicated 'dog water park' on the far east end where well-behaved dogs can swim completely off-leash.

Local Tip: Perfect for senior or small dogs due to the very shallow, calm water.

North ShoreLeash Required

Raccoon Street Dog Beach

Terrain: Rocky Shoreline

Located on the east end of Kings Beach State Recreation Area, just past the boat ramp. Placer County strictly bans dogs on the main sandy beach, so look for the signs.

Local Tip: Water shoes are highly recommended here due to the large, slick pebbles.

North ShoreLeash Required

Carnelian West Beach

Terrain: Flat Pebbles / Stones

A spacious, wide pebble beach right next door to Gar Woods restaurant. Includes lakeside picnic benches, BBQs, and year-round accessible restrooms.

Local Tip: Great spot to grab lunch to-go and sit right at the water's edge with your pup.

East ShoreLeash Required

Chimney Beach

Terrain: Sand & Massive Boulders

Located on National Forest land in Nevada. Named after the historic stone chimney remaining on the shore. It offers gorgeous turquoise alpine waters.

Local Tip: Requires a steep, roughly 1/2-mile hike down from the highway parking lot. Pack plenty of water!

East ShoreLeash Required

Hidden Beach

Terrain: Sandy Pockets & Granite Rocks

A stunning, secluded stretch just south of Incline Village along the East Shore Trail. Incredible crystal clear waters and deep swimming holes.

Local Tip: Accessible via the paved bike/walking trail. No immediate parking next to the sand.
